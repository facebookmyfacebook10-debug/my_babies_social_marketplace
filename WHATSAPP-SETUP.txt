MY BABIES WHATSAPP SETUP

1. Open index.html in a text editor.
2. Find: const WHATSAPP_NUMBER = "";
3. Enter the verified business WhatsApp number in international format, digits
   only. South African example: 27821234567 (no +, spaces or leading 0).
4. Save and test a product enquiry button. Opening WhatsApp does not complete
   an order; the customer still needs to review and send the message.

The number was not configured in the supplied files, so this project leaves it
blank rather than inventing a contact number.
